# soul-heir

Dungeon crawler where player plays as a fragment of a soul, which can inhabit/possess other bodies. Thus, when the body you are currently 'piloting' dies, your run doesn't end (yet), but you are instead expelled and need to find another host before you dissipate (at which point your run ends). 

# Story
You used to rule the kingdom, but an evil sorcerer duelled with you and shattered your soul into thousands of fragments. now he rules from a keep high on a mountain, and under his rule the world is falling apart: demons rising from hell, undead overflowing from cemeteries, beasts running wild.

## Core concepts
- fragmentation: each run you play as a different fragment of one overall. You were once very powerful, but as you have been split into so many parts you start very weak. During a run you may encounter other fragments of yourself. Each new run you start is a new fragment of the larger whole, so iterative runs are connected via some sort of shared awareness across runs.
- familiarity: as you spend time in a particular host, you gain familiarity with that type of host (e.g. human, animal, monster, demon, undead, etc). Thus, when you meet new hosts of that type, you will easier be able to live inside them and have better control over them due to previous familiarity.
- four elements: all things are composed of the four elements in some balance:
    - air: the soul. meta-physical. when you are expelled from a body, you exist as air.
        - associated with: cold, stable
        - over-represented in: humans
    - earth: the body. physical. bodies detached of souls return to earth
        - associated with: warm, stable
        - over-represented in: humans, undead
    - fire: the will. meta-physical. 
        - associated with: warm, volatile
        - over-represented in: demons, animals
    - water: change. physical.
        - associated with: cold, volatile
        - over-represented in: undead, demons
- species:
    - humans: earth + air
    - undead: earth + water
    - beasts: earth + fire
    - demons: water + fire
    - monster: water + air
    - ghosts: fire + air
- dream plane: you cannot stay awake forever, and when you sleep you enter the dream plane, an alternate realm where the soul can exist detached from the body.
- the cycle: when things 'die' their constituent parts are distributed. plants may spring up from dead bodies, the release of a fiery will might melt ice, etc. all spaces in the game are tracking the balance of different elements present at any one time.

## Themes
- stoicism/meditations: quotes from Meditations scattered throughout the game, examining the concepts of life, death, purpose.
- heavy metal / hard rock: rainbow, black sabbath
